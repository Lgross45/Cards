Sports cards Inventory System
## Project DescriptionThis project tracks sports cards boxes using QR codes, Google Forms, and Google Sheets.## How It Works.
1. Each item has a QR code.
2. Users scan the QR code.
3. Users fill out a Google Form.
4. Google Sheets records the transaction.
5. Inventory usage can be tracked over time.## Tools Used- Google Forms
- Google Sheets
- QR Codes
- Google Apps Script## Project GoalMake tracking and managing Sports cards easier and more organized.
